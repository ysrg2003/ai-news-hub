# Editorial Guidelines - AI News Hub

## Overview

This document outlines the editorial standards and guidelines for creating high-quality AI news articles on the AI News Hub platform.

## Content Standards

### 1. Article Structure

Every article should follow this structure:

- **Title** (50-70 characters): Clear, descriptive, and SEO-friendly
- **Excerpt** (150-160 characters): Compelling summary of the article
- **Introduction**: Hook the reader with a relevant question or statement
- **Main Body**: 3-5 sections with clear headings
- **Conclusion**: Summarize key points and provide actionable insights
- **Author Bio**: Brief description of expertise (50-100 words)

### 2. Writing Style

- **Tone**: Professional, informative, and accessible
- **Audience**: Tech-savvy but not necessarily experts
- **Language**: Clear, concise, and jargon-free (explain technical terms)
- **Paragraph Length**: 3-5 sentences per paragraph
- **Sentence Length**: 15-20 words on average
- **Passive Voice**: Limit to less than 20% of sentences

### 3. Content Quality

#### Minimum Requirements

- **Word Count**: 800-3000 words
- **Headings**: 3-5 main sections (H2)
- **Keywords**: Include primary keyword 2-3 times
- **Internal Links**: At least 2-3 links to related articles
- **External Links**: 2-3 authoritative sources
- **Lists**: At least one bulleted or numbered list
- **Code Examples**: Include when relevant
- **Quotes**: At least one expert quote or callout

#### Quality Checklist

- [ ] Title is compelling and SEO-optimized
- [ ] Excerpt accurately summarizes the article
- [ ] Introduction hooks the reader
- [ ] Content is well-organized with clear headings
- [ ] Paragraphs are concise and focused
- [ ] Technical terms are explained
- [ ] Examples are relevant and helpful
- [ ] Conclusion provides actionable insights
- [ ] Author bio is included
- [ ] All links are working
- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors
- [ ] Fact-checked and accurate

## Article Types

### 1. Trending Articles (🔥)

**Purpose**: Cover breaking news and trending topics

**Characteristics:**
- Time-sensitive
- Focus on latest developments
- Include expert reactions
- Published within 24-48 hours of news

**Structure:**
- What happened (hook)
- Why it matters
- Expert perspectives
- Implications and next steps

### 2. Evergreen Articles (📖)

**Purpose**: Provide lasting value and foundational knowledge

**Characteristics:**
- Timeless content
- Comprehensive guides
- Tutorial-style
- SEO-optimized

**Structure:**
- Introduction and context
- Step-by-step explanation
- Examples and use cases
- Best practices and tips
- Conclusion and next steps

## SEO Guidelines

### Keywords

- Include primary keyword in title
- Use secondary keywords naturally throughout
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Include long-tail keywords

### Meta Tags

- Meta description: 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword in meta description
- Make it compelling and action-oriented

### Internal Linking

- Link to 2-3 related articles
- Use descriptive anchor text
- Link to relevant categories
- Maintain natural flow

### Headers

- Use H2 for main sections
- Use H3 for subsections
- Include keywords naturally
- Keep headers descriptive

## Fact-Checking

1. **Verify Sources**: Check credibility of all sources
2. **Cross-Reference**: Confirm facts with multiple sources
3. **Check Dates**: Ensure information is current
4. **Verify Quotes**: Confirm accuracy of all quotes
5. **Update Links**: Ensure all links are working

## Plagiarism Prevention

- Write original content
- Properly cite and quote sources
- Use quotation marks for direct quotes
- Paraphrase with attribution
- Include proper citations

## Revision Process

1. **Self-Review**: Check for errors and clarity
2. **Peer Review**: Have another editor review
3. **Fact-Check**: Verify all claims and sources
4. **Final Edit**: Polish and publish

## Quality Scoring

Articles are scored on:

- **Readability** (0-100): Sentence and paragraph structure
- **Structure** (0-100): Organization and formatting
- **SEO** (0-100): Keyword usage and optimization
- **Engagement** (0-100): Lists, quotes, and emphasis
- **Overall** (0-100): Combined quality score

**Minimum Score**: 70/100 for publication

## Publishing Checklist

- [ ] Content meets minimum word count (800 words)
- [ ] Quality score is 70 or higher
- [ ] All facts are verified
- [ ] No plagiarism detected
- [ ] Images are optimized and credited
- [ ] Links are working
- [ ] Meta tags are complete
- [ ] Category is appropriate
- [ ] Tags are relevant
- [ ] Author bio is included
- [ ] Proofreading complete
